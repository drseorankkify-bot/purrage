Privacy Policy

How PurrAge handles information when you use the calculator and explore the site.

Last updated: 20-Sep-2026

Who we are

PurrAge (purrage.com) provides a free cat age calculator and related educational content about feline life stages. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Calculator inputs: The age you enter into our calculator is processed entirely in your browser and is not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on site, general location. This data is anonymized and aggregated.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to support basic site functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Contact form: If you use our contact form, we collect your name and email address solely to respond to your message.
  • Advertising: Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ads Settings.

How we use information

We use collected data only to operate, maintain, and improve PurrAge — understanding which content is useful and responding to inquiries.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, anonymized data with analytics or advertising providers as described above.

Changes to this policy
