The reference chart

Cat Age
Chart.

A 1-year-old cat is about 15 in human years, a 2-year-old is about 24, and every year after that adds about 4. So a 5-year-old cat is about 36, a 10-year-old is about 56 and a 15-year-old is about 76. The chart below lists every age from 1 to 25, with the life stage for each.

This page is the complete reference. To work out one cat's age, use the cat age calculator. For why the numbers work this way, see how do cats age.

Life stages follow the 2021 AAHA/AAFP guidelines, which count a cat as a senior from 10 years old. Age 10 sits in both the mature adult (7 to 10) and senior (10 and older) ranges, which is why it appears as a boundary. Our own cat life stages guide uses a six-stage version, so the names differ slightly between pages.

These are estimates for a typical cat. Breed, weight and other factors cause variation, so treat the chart as a guide rather than a measurement. Omni Calculator and The Old Farmer's Almanac publish similar 1–25 charts.

How to use the chart

  1. Find your cat's age in whole years. If your cat has just had a birthday, use that number.
  2. For ages between birthdays, add a share of the next year's 4. A cat aged 6.5 years is 24 + 4 × (6.5 − 2) = 42 in human years. The calculator accepts decimals like this.
  3. For kittens under 1 year, the whole first year counts as about 15, so the chart is too coarse. Use our kitten age chart.
  4. Check the life stage column to see which care guidance applies, and read the matching guide below.

How the conversion works

  1. Year one: the first year counts as about 15 human years.
  2. Year two: the second year adds about 9, so a 2-year-old cat is about 24.
  3. Every year after: each year adds about 4.

Worked examples: a 3-year-old cat is 24 + 4 = 28. An 8-year-old is 24 + (6 × 4) = 48. A 15-year-old is 24 + (13 × 4) = 76. The shortcut for any age from 2 up is 24 + 4 × (age − 2).

Human years to cat years

To go the other way, subtract 24 from the human age, divide by 4 and add 2. This works for human ages above 24. A 40-year-old is (40 − 24) ÷ 4 + 2 = 6 in cat years.

Why “multiply by 7” gets it wrong

The old rule multiplies a cat's age by seven. Compare it with the chart:

Cat age“×7” ruleChart aboveDifference
1 years715+8
2 years1424+10
5 years3536+1
10 years7056-14
15 years10576-29
20 years14096-44

For young cats the multiplier is far too low: a 1-year-old cat is closer to a teenager than a 7-year-old child. By around age 5 the two agree, and after that the multiplier overshoots, making a 20-year-old cat 140. Cats mature very quickly early on and age more slowly afterwards, so a single multiplier cannot fit both ends of life.

For kittens, use our kitten age chart, since a cat's first year covers a lot of development. When your cat reaches 10, read when is a cat senior.

If you don't know your cat's age

If you adopted a cat with no birth date, a vet can estimate age from the teeth, coat, eyes and body condition. Our guide to how to tell how old a cat is explains what each sign suggests. Use that estimate in the chart, and expect a range rather than an exact number.

What your cat's age means for care

  • Young adult (1 to 6): keep up annual exams, a healthy weight and good routines. See the cat feeding guide and healthy cat weight.
  • Mature adult (7 to 10): watch weight, teeth and mobility. See cat dental disease and cat arthritis signs.
  • Senior (10+): checkups at least every six months and a calmer home setup. See senior cat health, senior cat care and the senior cat diet.
  • Behaviour changes at any age can be the first sign of a health problem. See the cat behaviour guide.

Age is not the same as lifespan

The chart tells you how old your cat is in human terms, not how long it will live. In an English study of 4,009 cats, the median age at death was 14.0 years. Frequently asked questions

How old is a 10-year-old cat in human years?

About 56. Ten is also the age at which the 2021 AAHA/AAFP guidelines begin to count a cat as a senior.

How old is a 15-year-old cat in human years?

About 76, using 24 for the first two years plus 4 for each year after.

How old is a 1-year-old cat in human years?

About 15. A cat's first year counts for far more than one human year because cats mature so quickly.

How do you calculate a cat's age in human years?

Count the first year as 15, the second as 9 more (24 at age 2), then add 4 for every year after. The shortcut for age 2 and above is 24 + 4 × (age − 2).

How do I convert human years to cat years?

For a human age above 24, subtract 24, divide by 4 and add 2. For example, 56 human years is (56 − 24) ÷ 4 + 2 = 10 cat years.

At what age is a cat a senior?

The 2021 AAHA/AAFP guidelines define senior as 10 years and older. Senior cats should have checkups at least every six months.

Is the chart accurate for every cat?

No. It is an estimate for a typical cat. Breed, weight and other factors cause variation, so use it as a guide and talk to your vet about your cat's actual health.
